## Details

<a id="decision-1"></a>

### Keep the topic-centered pivot instead of restoring the introductory course

The current retained brief assumes EA familiarity and organizes around MacAskill’s sixteen menu items plus a transition introduction. The earlier eight-session introductory fellowship belongs in history/pre-pivot and must not be mistaken for the final design. See [idea/course-idea.md](idea/course-idea.md).

<a id="decision-2"></a>

### Treat per-topic reading maps as the retained deliverable

analysis/topics is the curriculum unit; cluster maps and the downloaded collection are supporting evidence. Preserve reading provenance and source frontmatter so selection can be revisited without presenting collection counts as proof of coverage or educational quality. See [README.md](README.md).

<a id="decision-3"></a>

### Keep research separate from claims about a running fellowship

This archived project retains research, stage reports and a publication pointer. Resuming curriculum work needs a fresh decision about learners and delivery; the historical pipeline does not establish current operation. See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).
